How Solar Panels Work

People often wonder how sunlight turns into electricity you can use at home. It happens in three simple steps:

  1. Sunlight Absorption: Solar panels on your rooftop absorb sunlight throughout the day using photovoltaic (PV) cells.
  2. Power Conversion: The panels generate Direct Current (DC) electricity, which is then sent to an inverter to convert it into Alternating Current (AC).
  3. Home Usage: The converted AC power flows directly into your home's main electrical board to run fans, lights, and appliances.

Environmental Benefits

Going solar is not just about saving money on bills. By generating your own clean power from the sun, you reduce your reliance on coal-based electricity, which significantly lowers your household's carbon footprint.

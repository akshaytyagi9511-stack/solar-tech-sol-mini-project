Tips to Maximize Your Savings

Once your solar system is installed, here is how you can make the most out of it:

  • Run heavy appliances like washing machines or water pumps during peak sunlight hours (10 AM to 3 PM) to use direct solar power.
  • Clean dust off your solar panels once every month to maintain maximum energy absorption.
  • Keep track of your monthly meter readings to ensure your system is performing at its best.
